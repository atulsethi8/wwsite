Where to stay in Phuket

Patong is convenient — but it is not for everyone.

Patong suits younger travellers who want nightlife and easy access to restaurants. Families or couples wanting a quieter atmosphere may prefer Kata, Karon or resort areas further away from the busiest streets.

Patong: nightlife and maximum convenience
Kata / Karon: calmer beach atmosphere
Premium resort areas: quieter, more self-contained stays
When to visit Thailand

Beach conditions vary by coast and season.

For Phuket and Krabi, the cooler/drier months are the most popular. Wet-season trips can still work well but sea conditions may affect island tours.

Boat tours are weather-dependent

Island routes may change or be cancelled when sea conditions are unsafe.

Nov–FebPopular period for Phuket and Krabi with generally more comfortable beach weather.
Mar–MayHotter conditions; beach holidays still popular.
May–OctWetter period on the Andaman side; attractive hotel value but more variable seas.
Thailand FAQ

Before you book.

Is Phuket or Krabi better?

Phuket has more hotel choice, nightlife and activities. Krabi feels more scenic and relaxed. Combining both is often the best first-trip solution.

Which Phuket area is best for young friends?

Patong is usually the most convenient for nightlife and walking access. Families and quieter couples may prefer Kata, Karon or resort areas further out.

Are island tours guaranteed to run?

No. Boat tours are subject to weather and sea conditions. Safety-related route changes can happen locally.

Can Bangkok be added?

Yes. Two or three nights in Bangkok can be added before or after Phuket/Krabi depending on flight routing.
