Sri Lanka FAQ

Before you book.

Is 5 nights enough for Sri Lanka?

Yes for a compact Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Bentota route. Seven nights is better if you want more leisure or an additional wildlife stop.

Can you arrange a scenic train ride?

Yes, subject to the route being operational and seat availability. We can retain the private vehicle for luggage and onward transfers.

Is Sri Lanka good for ladies groups?

Yes. Private transport, centrally located hotels and a balanced sightseeing plan make it easy for small groups.

Can we mix 4-star and 5-star hotels?

Absolutely. It is often the best way to improve the key stays without overspending across every night.
