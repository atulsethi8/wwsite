Why split your stay

Ubud and the coast deliver two different Balis.

Ubud works for rice terraces, temples, waterfalls and wellness. The southern beach areas are better for sunset, dining, water sports and resort time. A split stay gives you both without turning every day into a long transfer.

Seminyak or Nusa Dua?

Choose the beach area for your travel style.

Seminyak suits travellers who want restaurants, cafés and a livelier atmosphere. Nusa Dua is more resort-focused and often works well for families or travellers who want a quieter beach stay.

Seminyak: dining, cafés and nightlife
Nusa Dua: resort comfort and calmer pace
Ubud: culture, scenery and wellness
When to visit Bali

Bali works year-round, but the feel changes.

The drier months are popular for outdoor touring and beach days, while the wetter season is greener and can offer better hotel value.

Peak periods

School holidays, festive dates and popular summer months can push up villa and resort pricing.

Apr–OctGenerally drier and popular for sightseeing, beaches and outdoor activities.
Nov–MarWetter, greener period with tropical showers and often stronger hotel value.
Jul–AugHigh demand; book sought-after villas and family rooms earlier.
Bali FAQ

Before you book.

How many nights are enough for Bali?

Six to seven nights works well for a first trip with Ubud plus one beach area. Add nights if you want Nusa Penida, more leisure or a slower honeymoon itinerary.

Should I stay in Ubud and Seminyak or Ubud and Nusa Dua?

Seminyak is livelier with restaurants and cafés. Nusa Dua is more resort-focused and often suits families or travellers who want a quieter stay.

Are Bali tours private?

Our sample package is designed around private transfers and tours. Specific activities such as boat tours may be shared unless upgraded.

Can you arrange honeymoon villas?

Yes. We can compare private-pool villas, beach resorts and honeymoon inclusions within your budget.
