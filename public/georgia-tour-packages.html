Georgia FAQ

Before you book.

How many nights are enough?

Five nights works for Tbilisi plus Kazbegi/Gudauri. Seven nights or more is better if you add Batumi.

Is Georgia good for friends trips?

Yes. Tbilisi and Batumi can work well for nightlife-focused groups, while mountain day trips add variety.

Can you arrange hotels near casinos?

Yes. For nightlife or casino-focused trips, we can shortlist central hotels around your preferred area.

Georgia or Azerbaijan?

Georgia feels more mountain-and-café focused; Azerbaijan gives you a more polished modern-city experience in Baku. Both work well for short international breaks.
