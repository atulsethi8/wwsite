When to travel

There is more than one good time for Kenya.

July to October is the most famous period because of the Great Migration in the Mara, but Kenya offers rewarding wildlife viewing beyond migration season too.

Planning around the Migration?

We recommend booking well ahead for peak dates. River crossings are a natural event, so exact timing can never be guaranteed.

Jan–FebGenerally drier conditions and strong wildlife viewing; a good option outside the busiest migration months.
Mar–MayGreener, quieter and often better value, with more rain and some seasonal lodge considerations.
Jun–OctPopular dry-season safari period with easier wildlife spotting and high demand.
Jul–OctThe main period associated with wildebeest migration activity in the Masai Mara.
Safari conditions and migration timing vary from year to year. We plan around current seasonality and availability.

Kenya safari FAQ

Questions travellers ask us most.

How many nights should I keep for Masai Mara?

For most first-time visitors, 2–3 nights works well. Wildlife-focused travellers or premium conservancy stays may benefit from longer.

Should I do a road safari or fly into the Mara?

Road safaris usually offer better value and let you combine several stops. Fly-in safaris save travel time and work especially well for shorter or more premium trips.

Is the Great Migration guaranteed from July to October?

No. July to October is the main period associated with migration activity in the Masai Mara, but river crossings and herd movement depend on nature, rainfall and grazing conditions.

Can I combine Kenya safari with Diani Beach?

Yes. It is one of our preferred combinations for couples and families who want a relaxed beach finish after the safari.

Can Wander Wyze arrange flights and entry guidance too?

Yes. We can assist with international and domestic flights and guide you on the current entry requirements applicable at the time of booking.

Why do you not show one fixed price for every safari?

Kenya pricing changes significantly by travel month, park fees, camp category, vehicle type and whether you travel by road or air. We prefer quoting the current best-fit option rather than showing a misleading old headline price.
