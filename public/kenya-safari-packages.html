Best time for Kenya safari

Kenya is not only about July to October.

July to October is the best-known period because of migration activity in the Mara, but strong resident wildlife makes Kenya rewarding at other times too.

Planning around the Great Migration?

Book well ahead for peak dates. River crossings are natural events and exact timing can never be guaranteed.

Jan–FebGenerally drier conditions and good wildlife viewing outside the busiest migration period.
Mar–MayGreener and quieter with more rain; can offer better value depending on the lodge and route.
Jun–OctPopular dry-season safari period with strong demand and generally easier wildlife spotting.
Jul–OctMain period associated with wildebeest migration activity in the Masai Mara.

Kenya safari FAQ

Before you book a Kenya package.

How much does a Kenya safari package from India cost?

The cost changes significantly by season, flights, park fees, hotel or camp category, private versus shared vehicle, and whether you travel by road or domestic flight. Send us your dates and number of travellers for a current quote.

How many nights are ideal for Masai Mara?

For most first-time travellers, 2–3 nights works well. Wildlife-focused trips and premium conservancy combinations can justify a longer stay.

Is Kenya good for a honeymoon?

Yes. A premium Masai Mara camp followed by Diani Beach is one of the strongest safari-and-beach combinations for honeymoon or anniversary travel.

Road safari or fly-in safari — which is better?

Road safaris offer better value and make multi-stop itineraries easy. Fly-in safaris reduce transit time and suit shorter, premium trips.

Is the Great Migration guaranteed between July and October?

No. That is the main period associated with migration activity in the Mara, but herd movement and river crossings depend on natural conditions.
