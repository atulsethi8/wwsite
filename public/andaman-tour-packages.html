When to visit

Sea conditions matter.

Clearer weather and calmer seas usually make island transfers and water activities easier, while monsoon periods require more flexibility.

Never over-tighten the final day

Ferry and flight timings should have enough buffer, especially when returning from another island.

Andaman FAQ

Before you book.

How many nights should I keep on Havelock?

Three nights is a good minimum; four works better if you want scuba, leisure and multiple beaches.

Do ferry tickets need advance booking?

Yes, especially during peak periods. We plan hotels and ferries together so the route remains workable.

Is Neil Island necessary?

No. It is a nice slower stop, but travellers wanting fewer changes can spend longer on Havelock instead.

Can you add Baratang or stargazing?

Yes, depending on the overall duration, operating days and timing.
