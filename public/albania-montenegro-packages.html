When to go

Summer is lively; winter is quieter and more atmospheric.

Balkans FAQ

Before you book.

Albania + Montenegro or Montenegro + Croatia?

Albania + Montenegro can offer better value and a less-crowded feel. Croatia is stronger if you specifically want Dubrovnik or a more established Adriatic tourism route.

Is this good in December?

Yes for city hotels, old towns, food and a quiet couple trip. It is not a beach-season itinerary.

How do we cross between countries?

We can arrange private road transfers; exact border and entry requirements are reconfirmed for your travel dates.

How many nights in Kotor?

Two to three nights works well for most couples, with extra time if you want a very relaxed bay stay.
