When to visit

Vietnam weather changes by region.

There is no single perfect month for the entire country. We choose the route around the places you want to see and your travel dates.

North Vietnam

October to April is popular for cooler conditions. Summers are warmer and can bring showers.

Oct–AprPopular period for Hanoi, Ninh Binh and Halong with generally cooler conditions.
Feb–AugOften a useful window for Central Vietnam destinations such as Hoi An and Da Nang.
Year-roundVietnam can still be planned outside these windows by choosing the route carefully.
Vietnam FAQ

Before you book.

Is an overnight Halong cruise worth it?

Yes if you have the time. Day cruises are efficient; overnight cruises give a slower experience with sunset and early-morning scenery.

How many days are good for Vietnam?

Five to seven days works well for one or two regions. Ten days or more gives you room to combine north, central and south without rushing.

Can vegetarian or Indian-friendly meals be arranged?

Yes. We can communicate dietary preferences to hotels, restaurants and cruise partners, subject to what is available locally.

Can you include flights from India?

Yes. We can quote the land package together with suitable international and domestic flights based on your departure city.
