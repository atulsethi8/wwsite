Where should you stay?

The coast matters more than most travellers expect.

Different coasts can feel windier, livelier or more secluded depending on the month. We shortlist resorts around your travel dates, beach preference and whether you want nightlife or quiet luxury.

West: sunsets and easy access to attractions
North: restaurants and livelier areas
East: resort-led stays and scenic beaches
Best time for Mauritius

Warm year-round, with seasonal differences.

Mauritius is a year-round destination, but wind, rainfall and sea conditions vary by coast and month.

Hotel choice matters

The best-value resort is not always the best-located resort for your month.

May–OctGenerally cooler and drier; popular for comfortable sightseeing.
Nov–AprWarmer and more humid with tropical showers and warmer sea temperatures.
Festive datesChristmas, New Year and school holidays can carry premium resort rates.
Mauritius FAQ

Before you choose the resort.

Is Mauritius good for honeymooners?

Yes. It works especially well for couples who want a premium beach resort but also want sightseeing and activities outside the hotel.

Should I book half board or all inclusive?

Half board is often enough if you plan day trips. All inclusive works better when you expect to spend most of the time at the resort.

How many nights are ideal?

Six to seven nights is a strong first trip. Add nights for a slower luxury stay.

Can you arrange flights too?

Yes. We can package suitable flights with the resort and transfers for your exact departure city.
