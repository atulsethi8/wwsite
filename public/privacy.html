Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 July 2026

Wander Wyze Holidays respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information shared with us through our website, membership programme, enquiries, bookings and payment processes.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information including:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • City and address
  • Travel preferences and enquiry details
  • Membership and transaction information
  • Information necessary for travel-related services

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Process Wander Wyze Explorer Club memberships
  • Respond to travel enquiries
  • Provide membership benefits and travel-related services
  • Send booking, payment and membership confirmations
  • Provide customer support
  • Communicate offers and promotional updates where permitted
  • Meet legal, regulatory and accounting obligations

3. Payments

Online payments may be processed through authorised third-party payment gateways such as Razorpay. Wander Wyze Holidays does not directly store complete card, UPI or net-banking credentials.

4. Information Sharing

We may share relevant information with payment processors, hotels, airlines, transport providers, destination management companies, insurance providers and other service partners where necessary to provide the requested service.

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect personal information. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Cookies and Website Usage

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve website performance, understand visitor activity and enhance the user experience.

7. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably required to provide services, maintain business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Rights

You may contact us to request correction or updating of your personal information, subject to legal and operational requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

Wander Wyze Holidays may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any revised policy will be published on this page.
